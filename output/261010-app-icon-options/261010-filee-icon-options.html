Filee app icon: 10 options

Each concept is a 1024 px SVG on the macOS icon grid. Look at the 32 px and 16 px sizes and at the Dock strips, then reply with a number.

All 10 at a glance

The concepts

1

Twin Panes

Light desktop
Dark desktop

2 panes side by side: the left one active with a selected row, the right one dimmed like Filee's inactive pane.

The dual pane is the first thing you see when Filee opens, so the icon shows exactly that.

2

Red Folder

Light desktop
Dark desktop

A folder drawn the way Apple draws its own, in your red, with a split-view mark pressed into the front.

It sits next to Finder and System Settings without looking foreign. Up close, the mark says 2 panes.

3

F Rows

Light desktop
Dark desktop

An F built from a sidebar and 2 list rows. At 16 px it reads as F, at full size it reads as the layout.

A letter is the most compact mark there is, and this one also shows how Filee is laid out.

4

Swift Folder

Light desktop
Dark desktop

A white folder leaning forward, with 3 speed lines behind it.

Speed is the point of Filee: 10,000 files listed in 75 ms. The icon says fast before the app opens.

5

Key Cap

Light desktop
Dark desktop

The whole icon is a red key cap with a folder on top and a small ⌘ in the corner.

Filee is keyboard first: ⌘K, type-to-filter, a key for every command. The key cap says so.

6

Columns

Light desktop
Dark desktop

Miller columns, with the selection stepping down from column to column.

It shows how you drill into folders in Filee. The 3 red steps still read at 16 px.

7

Edelweiss Folder

Light desktop
Dark desktop

A white folder on your red, with the NicAI edelweiss on its front.

It keeps the link to today's icon and to NicAI, but now says files at first glance.

8

Graphite Line

Light desktop
Dark desktop

1 white line drawing of a folder split in 2, on graphite. No colour.

The quiet option: calm next to busy icons and at home in a dark Dock.

9

Peekee

Light desktop
Dark desktop

A red folder with 2 eyes, the ee of Filee, glancing right toward the other pane.

It matches the playful name and the -ee family. The eyes survive as 2 white dots at 16 px.

10

Command Palette

Light desktop
Dark desktop

The ⌘K palette: a search field with a red caret above a highlighted result.

Most work in Filee starts by typing. After the panes, this is the view you use most.
